Process

Three steps, no back-and-forth

Most scheduling tools show you free slots. MeetPlus decides which slot is actually fair, then handles everything downstream of that decision.

1

Connect your calendars

Google, Outlook or both — MeetPlus watches availability and time zones for everyone on the invite, not just you.

2

The AI proposes a time

It scores every open slot against each attendee's working hours and picks the one with the lowest total inconvenience.

3

Everyone gets a brief

Agenda, local time and dial-in land in each person's inbox in their own time zone — automatically, before the meeting starts.
